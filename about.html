About LSBU-ZeroDay

Our Mission

LSBU-ZeroDay is a student-led cybersecurity society at London South Bank University, dedicated to fostering hands-on learning, ethical hacking, and real-world security skills. We believe that every student—regardless of background—can contribute to a safer digital world.
